RAKETTI

A gravity cave flyer. Needs a keyboard.

Goal

Fly through the cave and collect every crystal. Then land gently back on the pad you started from. Your time starts when you lift off.

Land on any pad to refuel. Too fast or too tilted and you crash. The yellow arrow points to the nearest crystal, the orange one to a pad.

Keys

  • A / D or ← → turn
  • W, ↑ or Space thrust
  • R restart
  • N / P next / previous level
  • Z zoom
  • F fullscreen

Tips

Your best times are saved in this browser. If some keys do nothing, a browser shortcut or extension is catching them: try another browser.
